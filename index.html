Hawaii

The State of Hawaii is the 51st state in the United States. Hawaii has both the western most and southern most points of the U.S.A and is the most isolated populated land mass in the world. Hawaii's main industry is tourism, boasting miles of white stand beaches and lush mountain scapes the state truly defines paradise. Hawaii is a state that enjoys little variance in it's weather, as such agriculture is prominent in the islands. Coffee, cocoa, and pineapple are large industries not just for consumption but also for it's contributions to the tourism industry. Truly, Hawaii offers something to every person whether they seek adventure or relaxation.
