Featured Adventures

The Duct-Tape Dash

Our signature run. Big splashes, fast turns, and just enough chaos to make you laugh at your own screaming. Perfect for first-timers who want an exciting ride without committing to a full-day epic.

  • Duration: 2–3 hours
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Best for: Families, beginners, birthday bravery
Ask about this trip

Calm Waters Fishing Float

A slower adventure for people who want scenery, laughs, and a line in the water. We drift through calm sections, fish the bends, and enjoy the jungle views. If the guide looks worried, it’s probably nothing.

  • Duration: 2 hours
  • Difficulty: Easy
  • Best for: Groups, beginners, relaxing vibes
Ask about this trip

The Waterfall Lookout Run

A scenic jungle run built around a stunning waterfall viewpoint. This trip is more about the sights and the story than constant chaos. The river still moves fast in places, but you’ll have time to soak it in.

  • Duration: Half day
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Best for: Photos, nature lovers, “I want a story” people
